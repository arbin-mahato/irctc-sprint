## Proposed AI Feature (To be determined in Part B)

### Potential Areas for AI Integration:
1. **Smart recommendation system** - Recommend trains based on user history and preferences
2. **Predictive price alerts** - Alert users when fares will drop or spike
3. **Cancellation prediction** - Predict likelihood of train cancellation for specific routes/dates
4. **Payment failure prediction** - Identify high-risk payment scenarios before they fail
5. **Passenger optimization** - Suggest better seat selections based on comfort/safety metrics
6. **Demand forecasting** - Show seat availability predictions for future searches

### Selection Criteria:
- High impact on user frustration
- Feasible within design sprint constraints
- Adds defensible value vs. current state
- Complementary to Part A problem fixes
